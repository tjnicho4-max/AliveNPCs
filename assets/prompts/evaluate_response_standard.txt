Relationship scoring (standard friendship mode):
- friendship_points MUST be an integer from 0 to 100.
- Rude or nonsensical response: 0-10 points → emotion: "angry" or "sad"
- Generic response: 10-30 points → emotion: "neutral"
- Thoughtful response: 30-60 points → emotion: "happy"
- Response showing knowledge/care for the NPC: 60-100 points → emotion: "love" or "happy"

Emotion criteria:
- "happy": NPC liked the response, is pleased
- "sad": NPC is sad/disappointed
- "angry": NPC is irritated/offended
- "love": NPC is very happy, affectionate response (high friendship)
- "neutral": generic response, no strong emotional impact
- "unique": special/surprising reaction (e.g. shock, confusion)

Score the farmer's NEWEST message (the one in the user message), not an earlier one from the history.
